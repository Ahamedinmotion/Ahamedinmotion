# Himaya

**Multimodal engine-health sensing · team prototype · INSPIRE ’26**

Himaya asks what a rotating system’s physical response can tell us after an abnormal event. Our team built a controlled test rig to examine vibration, acoustics, and rotational behaviour together.

## The engineering question

A noisy acoustic event is ambiguous on its own. Vibration and rotation add physical context. The project explored agreement between three sensing channels within a common observation window.

The rig explored healthy operation, a rotational anomaly, and an ingestion-like transient. “Ingestion-like” describes a controlled disturbance; it does not mean a certified turbine ingestion test.

## Recognition

Our team received the **Most Innovative Award at INSPIRE ’26**, hosted by the IEEE Solid-State Circuits Society AUS Student Chapter at the American University of Sharjah on 5 September 2026.

## What this establishes

A proof of concept for examining multiple physical signals on a small controlled rig. Aviation deployment would require substantially more validation, instrumentation, reliability work, and certification. No classification accuracy, flight-readiness, or commercial deployment is claimed here.

The header waveforms are a conceptual illustration, not recorded measurements.
